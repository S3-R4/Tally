## Requirements

- Monthly 25-minute-per-day challenge, explicit enrollment, optional community sharing.
- One active personal custom challenge with positive days/minutes and today/tomorrow start.
- Own progress/history, completion badges, and opt-in wall of fame.

## Acceptance criteria

- All progress uses eligible focus and the group zone; no manual/edited/break time qualifies.
- Edits/deletion recalculate historical results/badges and opt-out removes shared visibility.
- Creating or joining is explicit and idempotent; duplicate definitions/participation cannot occur.

## Verification

- Enrollment/start boundaries, DST/month edges, qualification/recalculation, sharing/privacy, and history journeys.
