## Requirements

- Daily/weekly/monthly/yearly/lifetime reports, timeline/calendar/charts, tags/tasks and estimate/actual comparisons.
- Personal goals/streaks and saved time zone, theme, sound, and profile preferences.
- CSV/versioned JSON export, confirmed focus reset and account deletion with documented scopes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Totals agree across views and preserve DST/midnight/pause accounting.
- Manual/corrected focus counts personally; breaks and deleted records do not.
- Exports contain only owned records, escape CSV formulas, and omit tokens/other members' private fields.
- Reset/deletion affects exactly the documented records.

## Verification

- Aggregation/zone/export/isolation/reset tests, chart text alternatives, settings/report journeys.

- Changed: Personal daily/weekly/monthly/yearly/lifetime reports, timelines/calendars, counts/averages/best periods, task/tag totals and estimates, goals/streaks, profile/zone/theme/sound preferences, CSV/JSON export, confirmed reset/account deletion.
